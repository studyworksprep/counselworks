# Application readiness

Counselors open **Application readiness** from a student's navigation, essays or essay planning board. Students use **Application readiness** in their portal navigation; parents choose a linked student from their own navigation. Each college card brings together the application stage and round, calendar deadline, recorded checklist, assigned essay statuses, unassigned required library prompts, and open tasks connected to that application, its visible essays or its college workflow. Direct links lead to the college workspace, essay planning, essays and tasks. General essays appear separately, as do applications without a matching college-list entry, with a warning that their prompt requirements need review. Linked tasks that cannot be mapped to a displayed college are listed separately.

The dashboard is a read-only projection over the existing application, prompt, essay and task records; it creates no new data, workflow, notification, service client or migration. A missing checklist is explicitly unknown, incomplete required prompts are shown only to counselors and students, and unknown/unverified prompt coverage never implies that requirements are complete. Completed checklist items and approved essays do not imply that an application was submitted. Deadline attention uses the firm's calendar day and flags deadlines in the next 14 days or in the past, but does not flag applications already submitted, under review, decided or withdrawn. All-day due dates retain their date; timed task due dates use the existing instant formatter.

Parents see only family-visible essays and tasks and are explicitly told that this is a partial view. Unassigned prompt counts and titles are not shown to parents, because a private assignment may exist. Student-private and staff-private essay titles, related task details, story content and planning notes do not enter the parent page. Pending tasks remain staff-only until their owner is resolved. Staff access respects student assignment; portal access is checked against the signed-in student or parent relationship. Queries explicitly filter tenant, student, audience and archived/pending state even when the database service fallback bypasses row-level security.

Role acceptance covers counselor, student and both parents with shared and private essays/tasks, required prompt gaps, checklist and deadlines; it checks mobile layout and accessibility, plus the existing golden path and isolation scenarios. The unit test checks date boundaries and submitted/withdrawn exceptions. See the pull request for final validation and deployment status.
